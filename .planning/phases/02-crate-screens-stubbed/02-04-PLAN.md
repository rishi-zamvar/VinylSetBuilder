The thresholds are the V2 lesson made mechanical. `min > 110` means **no genuinely dark pixel
exists anywhere on the screen** — which is exactly what a mid-launch-fade capture looks like, and
exactly what the original V2 report was. `hi < 200` means no white ground. `lo == hi` is a blank
frame. Any of the three means the capture is not evidence and the settle delay must go up, not
the threshold down.

Record the full table of fifteen size/mean/min/max rows in the summary. Plan 02-05 compares
against it and it is the phase's measured visual artefact.
