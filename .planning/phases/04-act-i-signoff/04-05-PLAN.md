**The three verdicts**

- **SIGNED OFF** — S1…S7 all hold **and** zero rows at Medium or above are open.
- **SIGNED OFF WITH NAMED CARRY** — S1…S7 all hold, one or more Medium-or-above rows are open,
  and **every one of them is provably outside Claude's authority to close** (blocked on an
  unanswered owner question, or on a capability a later phase builds). Each carry is named,
  owned and reasoned. The roadmap's Phase 4 criterion is then reported **NOT MET, in those
  words**, rather than quietly re-worded.
- **REFUSED** — any of S1…S7 fails. Name exactly what is missing.

**No gate may be narrowed, filtered or re-thresholded to change which verdict is reached.**
If a gate is red, it is red. That is the rule V2 left behind, applied to this phase's own gates.

**Expected but NOT assumed:** V3 (Medium, blocked on owner question U8) and V12 (Medium, Phase 6
owns the fix) are both likely still open, which would make the verdict `SIGNED OFF WITH NAMED
CARRY`. Evaluate from the evidence this plan produces. `REFUSED` is a live outcome for any gate.

> *"Act ship criterion: committed screenshots of EVERY screen, taken in the booted simulator
> after a settle delay."*

**The phase-level criterion, from ROADMAP Phase 4, verbatim:**
